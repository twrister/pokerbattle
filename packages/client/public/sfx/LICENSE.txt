Sound effects (packages/client/public/sfx)
==========================================

All sound effects in this folder are from Kenney (https://kenney.nl) and are
released under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0) license:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 means these files are free to use in personal, educational and commercial
projects without attribution. Crediting Kenney is appreciated but not required.

Source packs
------------
- Interface Sounds (1.0)      https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
    ui_error, ui_announce, spawn
- Casino Audio                https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
    card_select, card_play, card_draw, pack_open, pack_claim
- RPG Audio                   https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
    melee, arrow
- Digital Audio               https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio
    result_draw
- Impact Sounds (1.0)         https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
    hit, hit_big, death, building_down

Files were renamed to match the game's sfx ids; audio content is unmodified,
except for the files listed under "Project-own replacements" below.

Project-own replacements
------------------------
These files no longer come from the Kenney packs; they are project-own audio
(already baked in, no attribution required). The Kenney entries for the same
ids above are superseded.

- explode, explode_big
    Two separate project-own explosion sources now: explode is 0.90 s (formerly
    the shared 1.03 s clip), explode_big is 1.64 s with a much deeper low end.
    Both are level-matched to the old explode (-21.0 LUFS integrated), so the
    bigger/louder feel of explode_big still comes purely from SFX_DEFS volume
    (0.55 vs 0.42). DC offset removed, trimmed, edge-faded, Ogg Vorbis.
- arrow
    Project-own bow release, attenuated by 8 dB.
- ui_click, ui_confirm, ui_back, ui_open, ui_close
    One shared click/pop source, peak-normalised to -1 dBFS, trimmed of trailing
    silence and mixed to mono. Confirm and back reuse the click sound verbatim;
    their louder feel comes from SFX_DEFS volume (0.42 / 0.38 vs 0.34).
- bomb_toss
    Project-own throw source (0.15 s), trimmed of trailing silence, mixed to mono
    and set to the same RMS as the previous sound (-18.3 dBFS), so the replacement
    is level-matched rather than louder.
- magic
    Project-own spell source (1.83 s, stereo). Light 2:1 RMS compression evens
    out its wider dynamic range, then the level is matched to the previous sound's
    short-term loudness (-13.7 LUFS median) and a look-ahead limiter holds peaks
    at -1 dBFS, so repeated casts stay clean instead of clipping.
- heal
    Project-own heal source (0.82 s mono at 22.05 kHz). Resampled to 44.1 kHz and
    raised 8.9 dB so its RMS matches the previous heal sound (-19.5 dBFS); peak
    lands at -4 dBFS, leaving headroom for the overlapping-voice case.
- result_win, result_lose
    Two separate project-own 5 s resolution cues replacing the previous 0.8 s
    stingers. Both are trimmed, peak-limited to -1 dBFS (the sources arrived at
    0 dBFS with slight clipping) and kept 1-2 dB below the old stingers: a 5 s
    cue holds attention far longer than a 0.8 s chime at equal loudness. The
    lose cue stays 1.1 dB quieter than the win cue, preserving the emotional
    contrast the two sources shipped with.

The thirteen files listed above come from project sources and need no attribution.
Every replaced file is 44.1 kHz, edge-faded (5 ms in; 15 ms out for UI, 30 ms for
bomb_toss, 60 ms for heal, 100-250 ms for the explosions, magic and the result
cues, whose tails run longer) and Ogg Vorbis encoded. Only the encoded .ogg files
live in this folder.
